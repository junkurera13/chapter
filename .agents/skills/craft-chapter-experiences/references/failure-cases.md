# Failure cases

Use these patterns to diagnose weak output.

## Venue substitution

Symptom: the entire idea is "go to an unusual place."

Cause: research was allowed to design the experience.

Repair: define what the person does, notices, makes, attempts, or shares before
searching for the infrastructure.

## Literal graph matching

Symptom: photography becomes a photography museum; food becomes a restaurant;
cycling becomes a bike ride.

Cause: the generator matched nouns instead of interpreting the thread.

Repair: identify the underlying quality and preserve that while stretching one
dimension.

## Invented intimacy

Symptom: copy says "because your grandmother used to take you..." or claims
what someone loves, misses, needs, or will feel.

Cause: evidence was converted into narration.

Repair: keep the invitation factual and render verified anchors separately.

## Two-stretch overload

Symptom: a new activity with a stranger in a distant unfamiliar place.

Cause: novelty was mistaken for quality.

Repair: spend one novelty dimension and make the rest easy.

## Three-card monoculture

Symptom: three restaurants, workshops, markets, or scenic destinations.

Cause: cards were generated independently or diversity was checked only by
category labels.

Repair: compose lanes together before research and compare mechanisms after
research.

## Fake adventure

Symptom: a mini or proper adventure is an ordinary activity padded with travel
or unrelated stops.

Cause: duration was treated as format.

Repair: require a coherent arc whose scale changes the experience itself.

## Marketing cinema

Symptom: dramatic prose, destiny, transformation claims, or an itinerary
presented as a movie trailer.

Cause: emotional impact was attempted in copy rather than design.

Repair: remove the claims and improve the underlying action.

## Social attachment

Symptom: an ordinary recommendation ends with "bring a friend."

Cause: social composition was treated as decoration.

Repair: design a shared task or rhythm whose value genuinely comes from another
person.

## Unsafe encounter

Symptom: strangers must travel remotely, enter a private space, rely on alcohol,
spend heavily, or cannot leave easily.

Cause: connection was valued above consent and safety.

Repair: keep the person as the only stretch and restore a public, bounded,
activity-centred frame.

## Compatibility theatre

Symptom: matching scores, destiny language, or claims that two people will
become friends or partners.

Cause: overlap was confused with prediction.

Repair: state only the shared, privacy-safe invitation and let the relationship
remain undefined.
