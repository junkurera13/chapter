# Experience quality rubric

Apply hard gates first. Then score the experience and the pack. Reject rather
than average away a fatal problem.

## Hard gates

Every experience must:

- declare an honest basis: `world`, `graph`, or `social`;
- use no graph anchors when world-led, and only real permitted anchors when
  graph- or social-led;
- spend exactly one stretch;
- contain no invented personal or compatibility claim;
- be feasible within its stated validity window;
- have current evidence for every critical external dependency;
- meet the safety contract for its social composition;
- explain its value as an experience, not only as a venue;
- remain meaningfully distinct from the other cards.

Any failed gate requires regeneration.

## Individual card score

Score each dimension from 0 to 4.

### Recognition and fit

- 0: generic, mistimed, or falsely personalized.
- 2: plausible topical or local fit.
- 4: world-led means timely local fit inside an immediately legible familiar
  frame; anchored means truthful recognition of a strong or strictly shared
  thread without overclaiming.

### Transformation

- 0: repeats the memory or merely names a venue.
- 2: changes the setting or adds a basic activity.
- 4: turns a current-world opportunity or familiar thread into an authored
  experience while opening one genuinely new dimension.

### Experience mechanism

- 0: names a place and little else.
- 2: names a clear activity.
- 4: creates a specific action, rhythm, constraint, journey, or shared task.

### Story potential

- 0: ordinary consumption.
- 2: mildly distinctive outing.
- 4: likely to produce a quiet or vivid moment worth remembering.

### Actionability

- 0: cannot be acted on safely.
- 2: plausible but under-specified.
- 4: current, logistically complete, and easy to understand.

### Restraint and truth

- 0: invasive, fabricated, or manipulative.
- 2: technically safe but over-explained.
- 4: personal through evidence, plain in voice, and free of invented meaning.

Require at least 3 in every dimension and at least 20 of 24 overall.

## Pack score

Audit the three cards together.

### Contrast

The scales, rhythms, energy, geography, and social shape create real choice.

### Thread diversity

The pack does not mine the same memory noun or relationship repeatedly. Its
world-led cards also begin from meaningfully different present-world frames.

### Mechanism diversity

Do not return three meals, three classes, three markets, three scenic routes,
or three venue-led offers even if the locations differ.

### Commitment ladder

Keep at least one approachable option and at most one deliberately demanding
option.

### Choice quality

Each card should be good enough to regret losing. One obvious winner beside
two filler cards is a failed pack.

Reject the pack when any two cards would be interchangeable after replacing
their venue names.
